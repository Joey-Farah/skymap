Hotels are on the map now.

Downtown's skyway-connected hotels — the Marriott City Center, the Westin, the Hilton, the Royal Sonesta and ten more — were in SkyMap all along: searchable, routable, connected. They just had no pin. Turn on Hotels and they were the one kind of place that didn't appear, which is a strange way to find out your hotel is in the app.

They have pins now, and so do the buildings that were quiet for the same reason: HCMC's Orange, Purple and Red buildings, the Ambulatory Outpatient Specialty Center, City Hall, the federal courthouse, and Westminster Presbyterian. Twenty buildings in all.

Thanks to the reader who wrote in about the Marriott. That report found a whole class of missing pins, not just one.

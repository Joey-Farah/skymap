Hundreds of places you couldn't find before.

Downtown's skyway-connected hotels — the Marriott City Center, the Westin, the Hilton, the Royal Sonesta and ten more — were in SkyMap all along: searchable, routable, connected. They just had no pin. Turn on Hotels and they were the one kind of place that didn't appear, which is a strange way to find out your hotel is in the app. They have pins now, along with HCMC's Orange, Purple and Red buildings, City Hall, the federal courthouse and Westminster Presbyterian.

Then a reader wrote in about a coffee shop in a hotel lobby we didn't have — and that one report turned up about a hundred more. Murray's, Cowboy Jack's, Monte Carlo, Gluek's, J.D. Hoyt's, Graze Food Hall, the Guthrie, Orchestra Hall, the Mill City Museum and dozens of others were all sitting in the map as unlabelled shapes. They're places again: searchable, filterable, on the map where they belong.

We also went looking for what nobody had written down anywhere. Spyhouse Coffee at the Emery, Socca and Mara at the Four Seasons, Aurora at the Marriott, Bep Eatery, 123 Sushi and Sushi Takatsu are all in SkyMap for the first time, each one checked against two sources.

And a few places that closed have been taken off the map, while four we'd marked as gone turned out to be very much open — so they stayed.

Thanks to the readers who wrote in. Both reports found a whole class of missing places, not just one.

Fresher places, and hours that stay right.

• Fifteen more places on the map, including Five Guys, Giulia, Champa Cafe, Lonely's Bar and the Portland Avenue Ramp — with six that have closed now removed.
• More opening hours say which days a place is actually shut, rather than leaving you to assume it keeps the same schedule all week.
• When a business publishes its own hours, those now win everywhere in the app. A restaurant saying it closes at 6:30 is no longer overruled by a map edit claiming 7:00 — being told a place is open when it isn't costs you the walk.
